FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about renting with RoarAway.

What do I need to rent a car? ▾
You need a valid US driver's license (or international license with translation), a major credit card in the driver's name, and you must be at least 21 years old (25+ recommended to avoid the young driver surcharge).
Are there any hidden fees? ▾
No. The price shown in your search results is your total cost including all taxes and mandatory fees. The only optional extras are add-ons you explicitly choose (insurance upgrade, GPS, child seat).
Can I cancel or modify my booking? ▾
Yes. All bookings include free cancellation up to 48 hours before pickup. Modifications can be made up to 24 hours before pickup through your online account or by calling us.
Is mileage included? ▾
All standard rentals include unlimited mileage within the continental United States at no extra charge. One-way interstate rentals and Alaska/Hawaii trips may have mileage caps — check your booking details.
What fuel policy applies? ▾
We use a full-to-full policy: pick up with a full tank, return with a full tank. If you return without filling up, a refueling fee at local pump price plus a small service charge applies.
Do I need additional insurance? ▾
Every RoarAway vehicle includes state-minimum liability coverage. Many personal auto insurance policies and premium credit cards also cover rental cars — check with your provider. We offer optional CDW (Collision Damage Waiver) at checkout for full peace of mind.
Can I pick up in one city and return in another? ▾
Yes! Select "One-Way Rental" when searching and choose different pickup and return cities. One-way fees vary by route and are shown transparently before you book.
What happens if I break down? ▾
Call our 24/7 Roadside Assistance line at 1-800-374-9111. We'll dispatch help to your location, arrange a replacement vehicle if needed, and cover reasonable towing costs for mechanical failures not caused by driver error.
