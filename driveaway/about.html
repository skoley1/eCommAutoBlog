Our Story

From One Car to 50 States

DriveAway started in 2015 when our founder, Marcus Webb, returned from a business trip furious about $180 in undisclosed fees on his rental car bill. He knew there had to be a better way.

We launched with a single fleet of 12 vehicles in Austin, Texas — with one rule: the quote you see is the total you pay. Word spread fast.

By 2019 we had expanded to 50 US cities. Today we operate 500+ locations across all 50 states, and we've served over 1.2 million drivers without ever adding a hidden fee.

Our Values

What We Stand For

Three principles that guide every decision we make as a company.

Radical Transparency
No hidden fees. No bait-and-switch pricing. What you see in the search results is your final total — always.
People Over Process
Real humans answer our phones. Our staff are empowered to fix problems on the spot — no scripts, no escalations.
Sustainable Driving
We're committed to having 40% of our fleet be electric or hybrid vehicles by 2027 to reduce our carbon footprint.
